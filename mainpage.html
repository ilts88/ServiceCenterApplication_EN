You are on the TSY service center website. If you experience technical problems while using our products, you can submit a warranty repair request. If the defect is recognized as a warranty case, we will repair your device. If repair is not possible due to the severity of the problem, we will replace your device with a new one.

To submit a repair request, go to the "Request for repair" tab. Fill in the repair request form with all the required information and click "Submit repair request" button. After submitting your request, the system will display your request ID. Save it to check your request's status later. To check the status of your request, go to the "Request status" tab, enter the request ID, and click "Submit" button. If you entered the ID correctly, the system will display the current status of your request. If our specialists have already processed your request by the time you check it, you will also see a comment from the service center.

We would like to inform you that our company undertakes to schedule a repair time and location within 7 days of submitting your request. We also undertake to complete the repair work within 30 days of submitting your request. Once your request is submitted, information about it will be stored in the system for at least 180 days.

According to our company policy, televisions and laptops are covered by a 2-year warranty. Household appliances, such as refrigerators and washing machines, are covered by a 3-year warranty. Air conditioners come with a 5-year warranty. Please note that repairs are performed at our company's service center (you will be provided with its address when scheduling a repair). We do not bear the cost of transporting the equipment to the service center, but we guarantee that you will be offered repairs at the nearest repair location. Air conditioners are an exception to this general repair policy, as transportation and dismantling them in the event of a malfunction is expensive. Therefore, our company will diagnose and repair your air conditioner at your home.

We also inform you that in the "News" tab, you can learn about the latest events related to TSY, such as our representatives' participation in forums and the publication of financial information. We also publish information in this section about upcoming changes to the company's operations and planned maintenance.
